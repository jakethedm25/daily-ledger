# 14-day trial plan

Goal: find out whether Daily Ledger fits into your life, not whether it is impressive.

## Rules

1. **No feature changes for the first 7 days.** Bug fixes that stop you from using the app are the only exception. Write ideas down instead (a list in your notes app).
2. Open it once a day, ideally at your anchor moment. Check in. Close it.
3. Do not edit the habit list during the trial, except the tiny version if it is wrong.
4. Export a backup on day 1 and day 7.

## Day 1 checklist

- Installed from the Home Screen icon, setup done there.
- Reminder set (Settings → Reminder: download the .ics, open it, add to Calendar).
- First check-in done.
- Export tested once (Settings → Export backup) so you know it works on your phone.

## Measuring

Settings → **Trial** shows the numbers from on-device session data. "Copy trial summary" copies a text block you can paste somewhere or into a chat. Nothing is transmitted.

- Days opened out of 14
- Median session length
- Check-ins per day, longest gap

## Verdict (decide on day 14)

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pass**: 10 or more of 14 days opened **and** median session under 60 seconds | Keep using it as is. Plan a v1.1 from your written ideas list. |
| **Middle**: 6 to 9 days opened, or median 60 seconds or more | Do not add features. Change one thing about the routine (anchor, reminder time, or habit tiny version) and run 7 more days. |
| **Hard stop**: fewer than 6 of 14 days opened | Stop. The app is not the right tool as built. Do not patch it. Return to the interview results and rethink. |

## Day 60 refresh

At day 60, reread the interview answers and the Settings → Trial summary. Ask: is the habit list still the right one, and is the reminder still at the right time? Adjust deliberately, then export a backup.

## Misses

A missed day is data, not a failure. The app offers the tiny version after one gap day and a gentle return after three. If you miss a few days, just open it.
